The Swift math follows UVR/sherpa STFT conventions. It was implemented for this project rather than vendoring their C++ implementation. Model parameter sources are linked in [models/README.md](models/README.md).

They are excluded from Git; separately versioned GitHub Release assets carry their own [model notices](models/MODEL_NOTICES.md) and are not relicensed under Apache-2.0. See [models/README.md](models/README.md). Listening excerpts in `samples/` retain the rights of their respective owners and are not licensed under Apache-2.0. Test signals are generated by this project's own code.
